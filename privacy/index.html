DuckLarry Privacy Policy

Effective: 22 October 2026 · Українська версія

Who is responsible

DuckLarry is made by Roman Shpylovyi, an individual developer based in Valencia, Spain. He decides how the personal data described here is used, and is its controller under the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR). In this policy, "we" and "us" mean him.

Contact: ducklarry.main@gmail.com

The short version

  • You can use DuckLarry without an account. Your words, answers, the duck's game and your settings then stay on your iPhone.
  • Speech is turned into text on your iPhone. Audio is never sent to us or to anyone else.
  • An account keeps your email address and the name Larry greets you by. With DuckLarry Pro, your library is also synced through our server.
  • Ask for help when stuck is off until you switch it on. When it is on, the text of what dictation heard and some of your saved words are sent to Claude, an AI service run by Anthropic.
  • No ads, no analytics, no tracking. We do not sell personal data or share it for advertising.
  • You can delete your account, and everything our server keeps with it, from inside the app.

What stays on your iPhone

The following is kept only on your device and is not sent to us:

  • your library while it is not synced: saved words, groups, every answer you give, coins, medals and the duck's game;
  • the name Larry greets you by, while you have no account;
  • your settings, the dictation corrections you make, and the speech model the app builds from your words to recognise them better;
  • a log of what dictation heard, and the recordings made by Dictation check in More, which exist to test recognition and are never uploaded;
  • reminders, which iOS schedules on the phone itself.

Deleting the app removes all of it; only two help-credit items in the iOS keychain outlive it (section 3). Deleting your account in the app erases it as well, with the exceptions listed under Deleting your account.

Speech and the microphone

DuckLarry uses the microphone only while you dictate a word. Speech is recognised by Apple's speech recognition running on your iPhone; the audio stays on the device. The only part of dictation that can leave the phone is the text described under Ask for help when stuck, and only when you have switched that on.

What our server receives, and why

Our server runs on Supabase. Its database is in Supabase's region in Ireland, in the European Union.

1. Your account (optional)

You can create an account with an email address and password, with Sign in with Apple, or with Sign in with Google. We then keep:

  • your email address (with Sign in with Apple this may be a private relay address Apple creates for you);
  • for an email account, your password, stored only as a secure hash;
  • for Apple or Google, the account identifier the provider gives us. Google also passes the name and the profile picture link on your Google account; our sign-in service stores them with the account, and the app does not use them;
  • the name Larry greets you by;
  • records our sign-in service keeps to run accounts securely: when the account was created and last signed in, and for each signed-in session its IP address and the type of device and app it came from.

Why: so you can sign in and keep your library under your account. Legal basis: performance of our contract with you (GDPR Art. 6(1)(b)).

2. Sync (DuckLarry Pro)

With Pro and an account, your library is copied to our server so that it is the same on all your devices:

  • saved words: the word, its part of speech, its meaning, topic and emoji, and when it was saved, changed or removed;
  • the groups you make, and which words are in them;
  • every answer you give in a sprint: which word, when, and how well;
  • events in the duck's game: sprints finished, things bought with coins, the fox, Larry rescued or hatched again;
  • for answers and game events, a random identifier of the device they came from, so that two devices can be merged.

Without Pro, nothing from your library is sent. If Pro ends, syncing stops; what is already on the server stays with your account until you delete it.

Legal basis: performance of our contract with you (Art. 6(1)(b)).

3. Help credits

Ask for help is limited to a number of questions a month, counted on our server. To know whose month a question belongs to, the app sends our server Apple's signed records:

  • the record that your Apple Account downloaded DuckLarry: the identifier Apple gives your Apple Account for this app — the same on all your devices and after reinstalling — the app version, the date of the first download, and a value Apple uses to tie the record to this device;
  • with Pro, the record of your subscription and, once the subscription has passed its end date while Apple still counts it as active (a billing grace period), Apple's signed renewal information for it; both are described in section 6.

The app sends them when it needs a new pass: when it starts while Ask for help is on; before it checks your credits (in More while Ask for help is on, in the coins sheet, or when Pro starts or ends — even with Ask for help off); with a question or a check of your credits when there is no pass, it has run out, or it is within ten minutes of its end; at the next question or check after your subscription changes (it starts, renews or ends); and when our server does not accept the pass. After an attempt that fails, the app waits up to ten minutes before the next — never past the end of the pass it still has — unless your subscription changes.

Our server checks Apple's signatures and does not store these records. From them it issues a pass saying, for up to a week and never past the end of the subscription or of Apple's billing grace period for it, which Apple identifier you are and whether you have Pro. The app keeps the pass in the iOS keychain and sends it with every question and every check of your credits.

The app also keeps a random identifier of its own in the keychain and sends it, with whether you have Pro, alongside the pass. When our server cannot check Apple's records — in a test build, for example — the month is counted against this identifier instead.

The pass and the app's own identifier stay in the iOS keychain when you delete the app and when you delete your account, so that neither starts the month's count again. They hold nothing but the identifiers, whether you have Pro and until when, and, with Pro, Apple's number for the subscription purchase and the dates it ends.

For each identifier and month, the server keeps only how many questions were used and how many credits were obtained for coins. Apple's identifier is a pseudonym: our server knows nothing else about the Apple Account behind it. Neither identifier is linked to your DuckLarry account, your name or your email, and the record does not contain what you asked.

Why: the AI service costs money per question, and the monthly limit has to be counted where it cannot be changed from the phone. Legal basis: our legitimate interest in limiting cost and preventing abuse (Art. 6(1)(f)), and performance of our contract with you (Art. 6(1)(b)).

Because the record belongs to an identifier and not to an account, deleting your account does not delete it, and the app does not show the identifiers, so we cannot find a person's record by name or email. Each month's count is kept for that month and the twelve after it, and then deleted automatically.

4. Ask for help when stuck

This setting is off until you switch it on in More. When it is on and dictation cannot work out what you said in either of your languages, the app sends to our server:

  • the text of the two readings of what dictation heard, and up to four alternative readings of each;
  • the language you are learning and your own language;
  • up to 40 words from your library, as context for what you are likely to say;
  • the pass, the app's own identifier and whether you have Pro (section 3), which stay on our server.

Our server forwards the readings, the languages and the library words to Claude, an AI model made by Anthropic, and returns its answer. The app checks every word Claude suggests against its dictionary before you see it.

Never sent: audio, your name, your email address or your account.

We do not store the text of the question or of the answer. Our server records only how large each question was, to keep track of the cost.

Anthropic acts as our processor under its commercial terms. It does not use this data to train its models. It deletes the input and the output within 30 days, except that it may keep them for up to 2 years if its safety systems flag a request as breaking its Usage Policy, or longer where the law requires. Anthropic processes the data in the United States.

Legal basis: your consent (Art. 6(1)(a)), given by switching the setting on. You can withdraw it at any time by switching it off; questions already sent are not affected.

5. Dictionary and language downloads

The app downloads translation packs and, with Pro, the rest of the dictionary from our server. When you look up a rare word that is not on your phone, the app asks our server for it: that request contains the word, but no account and no identifier.

As with any request on the internet, our hosting provider sees your IP address, and keeps technical logs of requests for a short time to run and secure the service.

Legal basis: performance of our contract with you (Art. 6(1)(b)) and our legitimate interest in keeping the service secure (Art. 6(1)(f)).

6. Subscriptions and payments

DuckLarry Pro is sold and billed by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive your payment details, your Apple ID, or the name and email address of your Apple Account.

So that Pro's help credits apply, the app sends our server Apple's signed record of your subscription (section 3): the product, when it was bought and when it ends, its price and currency, the App Store country, and Apple's numbers for the purchase. Our server checks Apple's signature, reads whether the subscription is active and until when, and does not store the record.

If the subscription has passed its end date but Apple still counts it as active while it tries to take the payment (a billing grace period), the app also sends Apple's signed renewal information for it: whether, to which plan and when it renews, the renewal's price and currency, any offer or consent to a price increase that goes with the renewal, why it stopped if it did (for example, a failed payment), whether Apple is still trying to bill, when the grace period ends, when the current run of the subscription began, whether it is a real or a test purchase, Apple's numbers for the subscription, and the identifier Apple gives your Apple Account for this app (the same as in the download record). Our server checks Apple's signature, reads when the grace period ends, and does not store it.

Apple's privacy policy applies to the purchase. You can manage or cancel the subscription in your iPhone's Settings.

  • Anthropic: the Claude AI model behind Ask for help, in the United States, under Anthropic's commercial terms and data processing addendum.

Apple and Google are responsible on their own account for their part: Apple for Sign in with Apple, the App Store and the iPhone; Google for Sign in with Google, which happens on Google's own page under Google's privacy policy.

Transfers outside the EU

Anthropic is in the United States, and Supabase and the companies it relies on may handle data from outside the European Economic Area. These transfers are protected by the safeguards in each provider's data processing terms, such as the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses.

How long we keep data

  • Account and synced library: until you delete the account. Deletion on our server is immediate; copies in our hosting provider's backups disappear when those backups expire.
  • Help-credit counts: the month in progress and the twelve before it; older months are deleted automatically.
  • Ask for help: not stored by us; Anthropic as described in section 4.
  • Technical logs: kept by our hosting provider for a short period.

Deleting your account

In the app: More → your profile at the top → Delete account. This deletes your account and everything our server keeps with it — your email, name, sign-in records and synced library — and then erases DuckLarry's data on that phone, except a few things that are not about your learning:

  • the two help-credit items in the iOS keychain (section 3) and the app's own note of your help credits, of whether you have Pro, and of until when our server said the day's help budget was used up — kept so that counts and status do not start over;
  • the install identifier (section 2) — kept so that the library the app has open is not written under two identifiers;
  • the downloaded dictionary and translation packs, which are the same for everyone.

On your other devices, delete the app to remove what is stored there.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to access your data, to correct it, to have it deleted, to restrict or object to its use, to receive it in a portable form, and to withdraw consent at any time. In the app you can change your name and delete your account in More → your profile, and switch off Ask for help in More. For anything else, write to the contact address above; we answer within one month.

You also have the right to complain to a data protection authority: in Spain, the Agencia Española de Protección de Datos (aepd.es), or the authority where you live.

Children

DuckLarry has no minimum age, and can be used without an account, with everything kept on the phone. Creating an account and switching on Ask for help do send personal data to our server and, for Ask for help, to Anthropic. Below a certain age a person cannot agree to that alone: 14 in Spain, and between 13 and 16 elsewhere in the EU, depending on the country. If you are under that age, ask a parent or guardian before doing either. A parent who finds that a child has done so without permission can delete the account in the app, or write to us and we will delete it.

Security

Everything travels over encrypted connections. On our server, each person's data can be read only by their own signed-in account, and the keys that bypass this never ship in the app.

Changes

When this policy changes, the date at the top changes. If a change affects what leaves your phone, the app will tell you before it applies.

3. Кредити допомоги

Кількість запитань у «Просити допомоги» обмежена на місяць і рахується на нашому сервері. Щоб знати, чий це місяць, застосунок надсилає на сервер підписані записи Apple:

  • запис про те, що ваш акаунт Apple завантажив DuckLarry: ідентифікатор, який Apple дає вашому акаунту Apple для цього застосунку, — однаковий на всіх ваших пристроях і після перевстановлення, — версію застосунку, дату першого завантаження й значення, яким Apple привʼязує запис до цього пристрою;
  • з Pro — запис про вашу підписку, а коли дата її закінчення минула, але Apple ще вважає її активною (пільговий період оплати), — ще й підписану інформацію Apple про її подовження; обидва описано в розділі 6.

Застосунок надсилає їх, коли йому потрібна нова перепустка: під час запуску, якщо «Просити допомоги» ввімкнено; перед перевіркою кредитів (у «Більше», якщо «Просити допомоги» ввімкнено, у вікні монет або коли Pro починається чи закінчується — навіть якщо «Просити допомоги» вимкнено); із запитанням чи перевіркою кредитів, коли перепустки немає, вона спливла або до її кінця лишається менше десяти хвилин; із наступним запитанням чи перевіркою після зміни підписки (вона почалася, подовжилася чи закінчилася); і коли сервер не приймає перепустку. Після невдалої спроби застосунок чекає до десяти хвилин до наступної — не довше, ніж діє перепустка, яка в нього є, — якщо тільки підписка не змінилася.

Сервер перевіряє підписи Apple і цих записів не зберігає. З них він видає перепустку, яка до тижня — і не довше, ніж діє підписка або пільговий період оплати для неї, — каже, який ви ідентифікатор Apple і чи є у вас Pro. Застосунок тримає перепустку у звʼязці ключів iOS і надсилає її з кожним запитанням і кожною перевіркою кредитів.

Ще застосунок тримає у звʼязці ключів власний випадковий ідентифікатор і надсилає його разом із перепусткою, а також те, чи є у вас Pro. Коли сервер не може перевірити записи Apple — наприклад, у тестовій збірці, — місяць рахується на цей ідентифікатор.

Перепустка й власний ідентифікатор застосунку лишаються у звʼязці ключів iOS, коли ви видаляєте застосунок і коли видаляєте акаунт, щоб жоден із цих кроків не починав лічбу місяця заново. Вони містять лише ідентифікатори, те, чи є у вас Pro, і до коли, а з Pro — номер покупки підписки в Apple і дати її закінчення.

Жоден із ідентифікаторів не повʼязаний з вашим акаунтом DuckLarry, імʼям чи поштою, а запис не містить того, про що ви питали.

4. Просити допомоги, коли не виходить

  • текст двох варіантів того, що почуло диктування, і до чотирьох альтернативних варіантів кожного;
  • мову, яку ви вивчаєте, і вашу мову;

Діти

DuckLarry не має мінімального віку, і ним можна користуватися без акаунта, коли все зберігається на телефоні. Створення акаунта й увімкнення «Просити допомоги» надсилають персональні дані на наш сервер, а для «Просити допомоги» — ще й до Anthropic.
